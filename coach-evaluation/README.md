# Coach evaluation workspace — 10Guillot (Gio)

Working folder supporting the coach/moderator evaluation that follows from the
Arbitration Committee ruling in
[Deblokkade 10Guillot (3)](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Arbitragecommissie/Zaken/Deblokkade_10Guillot_(3)).

The standard being evaluated (from the ruling):

> Hij mag geen nieuwe artikelen aanmaken totdat de problemen met al zijn oude,
> door hem gestarte artikelen zijn opgelost en in ieder geval niet eerder dan de
> coach en moderators daar toestemming voor hebben gegeven.

The original complaint underlying the 2022 block concerned violations of the
guidelines on **neutral point of view, original research and verifiability**
across articles he started. This workspace therefore tracks two things:

1. **`articles_under_review.md`** — all mainspace articles Gio started or
   significantly expanded, i.e. the population the "problemen … opgelost"
   condition applies to. Generated by `collect_articles.py`; the assessment
   columns are filled in by hand (or in review sessions), not by the script.
2. **`interactions_since_unblock.md`** — how Gio has interacted with his
   mentor/coach and with the wider community since the ArbCom unblock.
   Generated by `collect_interactions.py`.

## Conventions

All collection follows the repo's agreed fetching restrictions
(see `wikimedia-coding-agent-lessons`):

- **API-first, never scrape** — everything goes through `https://nl.wikipedia.org/w/api.php`.
- Descriptive **User-Agent** with contact + purpose (see `config.py`).
- **`maxlag=5`** on every request; automatic back-off when the cluster is lagged.
- One `requests.Session` per host, small delay between paginated calls.
- **Provenance stamping**: every JSON snapshot in `data/` records the endpoint,
  exact params, and a UTC timestamp, so results are reproducible.

## Usage

```bash
python3 collect_articles.py            # → data/articles_<date>.json + articles_under_review.md
python3 collect_interactions.py        # → data/interactions_<date>.json + interactions_since_unblock.md
python3 evaluate_articles.py           # → evaluation/<article>.md sheets + evaluation/INDEX.md
python3 collect_interactions.py --since 2025-05-22T00:00:00Z   # override auto-detected unblock date
```

The evaluation goal and method are written up in **`coach_prompt.md`**:
one pass over all articles (what Gio added since the ruling, whether the
improvements included proper sources, policy alignment, and what people
complained about) plus a conduct pass over mentor/community interaction.

`collect_interactions.py` auto-detects the most recent unblock from the public
block log; pass `--since` to override. Set `MENTOR_USERS` in `config.py` once
the mentor/coach usernames are confirmed so mentor interaction is split out
from general community interaction.

> **Note:** running these from a sandboxed session requires `nl.wikipedia.org`
> to be on the network egress allowlist. The scripts fail fast with a clear
> message when the proxy refuses the connection.

The generated lists are working documents for the coach evaluation. They record
publicly available on-wiki activity only, and they describe — they don't judge;
assessment happens in the marked columns and in discussion.
